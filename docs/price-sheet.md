# Acme Solar Price Sheet

Acme Solar is a fictional company and these prices are synthetic demo content. Prices are valid from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027, include GST, and already have the federal small-scale rebate taken off.

## 1. Solar packages
- 6.6 kW system (15 panels): $5,490
- 8.8 kW system (20 panels): $6,990
- 10.4 kW system (24 panels): $8,290

## 2. Batteries
- 10 kWh battery, installed: $8,900
- 13.5 kWh battery, installed: $11,400
- Backup gateway (keeps power on during a blackout): $1,200 extra
Adding a battery to a system we installed earlier costs an extra $450 if the inverter is not battery-ready.

## 3. Extras
- Tilt frames for flat roofs: $45 per panel
- Two-storey roof surcharge: $350
- Switchboard upgrade: $650 to $1,100, quoted after the roof assessment
- Bird-proofing mesh around the panels: $590

## 4. Payment
A 20 percent deposit is due when you sign. The balance is due within 7 days of completion. Card payments carry a 1.2 percent surcharge; bank transfer has no fee. A 0 percent interest payment plan over up to 24 months is available through our partner lender, with a $99 establishment fee and subject to approval.

## 5. Discounts
Seniors Card holders get 5 percent off any solar package. If you refer a friend, we pay you a $250 referral credit once their system is installed. Discounts cannot be combined.

## 6. Cancellations and refunds
You have a 10 business day cooling-off period after signing, during which you can cancel and get your full deposit back. After the cooling-off period, if you cancel before we order your equipment, we refund the deposit minus a $300 design fee.

## 7. What every price includes
Panels, inverter, mounting, installation, the grid connection application, the Acme Solar app, and the 5-year workmanship warranty.
