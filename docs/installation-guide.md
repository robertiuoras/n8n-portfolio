# Acme Solar Installation Guide: What to Expect

Acme Solar is a fictional company. This guide is synthetic demo content.

## 1. Before installation day
Every job starts with a free roof assessment that takes about 45 minutes. We then send a system design and a written quote, which stays valid for 30 days. A 20 percent deposit confirms the booking. Before we can install, your electricity network has to approve the connection; that approval usually takes 2 to 6 weeks and we lodge it for you.

## 2. Preparing your home
Please keep the driveway clear for our truck, make sure we can reach the switchboard, keep pets inside, and trim any branches that hang over the roof. Someone over 18 needs to be home when the team arrives and again at handover.

## 3. On installation day
A team of three arrives between 7am and 8am. Your power will be switched off for about 2 hours while the electrician works on the switchboard. Most systems are finished in one day. Systems larger than 10 kW, and homes with two-storey roofs, take two days.

## 4. Switchboard upgrades
Older switchboards with ceramic fuses must be upgraded before a solar system can be connected. We spot this during the roof assessment. A switchboard upgrade costs between $650 and $1,100 and is quoted separately.

## 5. Commissioning and handover
Before we leave, the electrician tests the system and sets up the Acme Solar app with you. Your Certificate of Electrical Safety is emailed within 5 business days. Your electricity retailer then installs a two-way (bidirectional) meter, which can take up to 3 weeks after installation. Until that meter is in, the power you send to the grid is not credited to your bill.

## 6. Roof types
We install on tile roofs, metal roofs and flat roofs. Flat roofs need tilt frames, which cost an extra $45 per panel. We cannot install on roofs that contain asbestos; the roof has to be replaced first.

## 7. After installation
We call you two weeks after installation to check everything is working as expected, and we do a free system health check at the 12-month mark.
