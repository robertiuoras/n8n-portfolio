# Acme Solar Privacy Policy

Acme Solar is a fictional company. This policy is synthetic demo content.

## 1. What we collect
We collect your name, phone number, email address, street address, photos of your roof, any electricity bills you send us, and production data from your inverter once your system is running.

## 2. Why we collect it
We use this information to prepare quotes, plan and complete the installation, handle warranty claims and run the Acme Solar app. We use production data to spot faults early, for example a panel string that has stopped producing.

## 3. Who we share it with
We share only what each party needs: the partner finance lender (only if you apply for a payment plan), your electricity network (for the grid connection approval), the installation team, and our cloud hosting provider, which stores all data in Australia. We never sell your personal information, and we do not share it with marketing companies.

## 4. How long we keep it
Customer records are kept for 7 years after our last contact with you, because tax law requires it. Production data is kept for as long as your warranty runs. If you only asked for a quote and never went ahead, we delete your details after 2 years.

## 5. Marketing messages
We send our newsletter only if you opt in, and every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. Text messages are used only for appointment reminders.

## 6. Your rights
You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you by emailing privacy@acmesolar.example. We answer within 30 days. If you are not happy with how we handled a privacy complaint, you can take it to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

## 7. Cookies
Our website uses cookies for basic visit statistics. You can turn them off in your browser settings and the site will still work.
