# Acme Solar Warranty Terms

Acme Solar is a fictional company. These terms are synthetic demo content.

## 1. Who these terms cover
These terms cover every home solar and battery system installed by Acme Solar in Queensland and northern New South Wales on or after 1 July 2025. The warranty stays with the house, not the person. If you sell the house, the warranty can be transferred to the new owner once. To transfer it, the new owner must email us within 60 days of settlement with proof of sale and the system ID.

## 2. Panel warranty
Panels carry a 25-year product and performance warranty. Output may drop by up to 2 percent in the first year and by no more than 0.4 percent a year after that. At year 25 every panel is guaranteed to produce at least 87.4 percent of its original rated output. If a panel falls below that line we replace it.

## 3. Inverter warranty
Inverters carry a 10-year warranty from the installation date. You can extend the inverter warranty to 15 years for a one-off fee of $349, but only if you buy the extension within 90 days of installation.

## 4. Battery warranty
Batteries are covered for 10 years or 37.8 MWh of energy throughput, whichever comes first. At the end of the warranty the battery is guaranteed to hold at least 70 percent of its original capacity.

## 5. Workmanship warranty
Our own workmanship is covered for 5 years. That includes roof penetrations, roof leaks caused by the installation, cabling, conduit and the mounting rails.

## 6. What is not covered
The warranty does not cover damage from storms stronger than the panel's rated wind load, hail stones larger than 35 mm, pest damage such as possums or rats chewing cables, changes or repairs made by anyone other than Acme Solar, damage from cleaning with a high-pressure washer, or panels that a third party has removed and refitted (for example during re-roofing).

## 7. How to make a claim
Email warranty@acmesolar.example with your system ID (shown on the Settings page of the Acme Solar app), photos of the problem and a short description. We acknowledge every claim within 2 business days and book a site visit within 10 business days. During the 5-year workmanship period, approved repairs cost you nothing, labour included. After that, parts under a product warranty are still free but a $120 call-out fee applies to the visit.

## 8. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law
These warranties are in addition to the rights you have under Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in these terms limits those rights.
